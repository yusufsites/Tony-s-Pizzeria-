About Us

Welcome to Tony's Pizzeria, where tradition meets passion. For over 20 years, we've been serving Austin's finest authentic Italian pizza, crafted using time-honored recipes and the freshest ingredients. Our wood-fired ovens reach temperatures of 900°F, creating the perfect crust with a crispy exterior and tender interior.

Every pizza is a masterpiece, prepared by our skilled pizzaiolos who take pride in their craft. We source our ingredients from trusted suppliers, ensuring quality in every bite. From our family to yours, we invite you to experience the warmth, flavor, and hospitality that makes Tony's Pizzeria a beloved Austin institution.
